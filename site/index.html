Questions from the viewing room

Are Dead Purple printables a digital download?

Yes. The horror wall art, logic puzzles, party games and budget planners are digital files sold through the Dead Purple Whop store. You download them after checkout and print at home or at a print shop. Nothing ships.

What sizes do the horror wall art prints come in?

The printable art is made at 18x24 inches at 300 DPI and can be cropped down to 16x20, 12x16 or 8x10.

Does the 2027 budget planner work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes. The Dead Purple 2027 Budget Planner is a spreadsheet built for both Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel.

Where do I buy Dead Purple t-shirts?

Mortician humor and Gothmas tees are printed and shipped by TeePublic from the Dead Purple TeePublic shop.

Is there a free Halloween party printable?

Yes. Join the email list and you get the free Spooky Party Starter PDF with three morgue-mystery logic puzzles and six icebreaker cards.

Can I use the pet ofrenda for a dog or cat?

Yes. The Pet Ofrenda Printable is a pet memorial frame with paw prints, a photo window, and lines for a name and dates, so it works for a dog, a cat, or any pet. It is a bilingual English/Spanish PDF in US Letter and A4, made for a Día de los Muertos ofrenda (Nov 1-2) or as a year-round pet memorial.

Are the toe tag gift tags printable at home?

Yes. The Maison du Morgue Toe Tag Gift Tags are an instant PDF download with 8 tags per US Letter page and fill-in To / From lines. Print at home as many times as you like, cut them out, punch a hole and add twine. Digital file only; nothing ships.
